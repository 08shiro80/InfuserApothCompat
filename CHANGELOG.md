# Changelog

## 1.3.6

### Fixes
- **A plain vanilla bookshelf next to the infuser no longer breaks the enchantment/infusion display.** With an ordinary bookshelf among the enchanting shelves, the infuser could stop detecting infusion recipes and the Jade tooltip could stop showing stats. Vanilla bookshelves now simply contribute their power like they should.
- **Bookshelves behind a non-solid block now count toward the infuser's power** (glass, glass pane, fence, slab, …), matching the Enchanting Infuser itself, and the Jade tooltip matches the power actually used.
- **Fixed the enchantment list disappearing after viewing a JEI recipe.** Pressing "U" (uses) on an item in the infuser and returning to the GUI cleared the whole enchantment list until the GUI was reopened. The list now stays.

## 1.3.5

### Features
- Apotheosis / Apothic Enchanting integration for the Enchanting Infuser on Minecraft 26.1.2: bookshelf Eterna powers the infuser, treasure and filtering shelves are respected, Apotheosis infusion recipes can be crafted in the infuser, and the infuser appears in JEI for those recipes.
